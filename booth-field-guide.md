# Booth Field Guide
*What the photo booth will and won't do for the person in front of the camera.*

## The roster

Up to **5** animations can be active at once, picked from the bar above the camera.

| Animation | How it behaves |
|---|---|
| Owl | Perches on your arm |
| Bat | Perches on your arm |
| Globe | Orbits around you |
| Drone | Hovers over your palm |
| Scuba | Follows your hands |
| OC Fusion | Follows your hands |
| Pig Nose & Ears | Face overlay |
| Bat Ears | Face overlay |
| Clown Wig & Nose | Face overlay |
| Sunglasses | Face overlay |
| Mustache | Face overlay |
| 67 | Lands on your palm |

Tap a animation to turn it on, tap again to turn it off. Mix and match freely, up to five at a time — past that, every other button greys out until one is turned off.

> **Note:** Face overlays (Pig Nose & Ears, Bat Ears, Clown Wig & Nose, Sunglasses, Mustache) all sit on the same face. Turning on more than one or two at once isn't blocked, but they'll overlap and crowd each other — not recommended.

## Two pairs don't mix

| Pair | Why |
|---|---|
| Owl **×** Bat | Both land in the exact same spot on your forearm as picking both stacks two creatures on one arm instead of giving you two. |
| Drone **×** 67 | Both trigger off the same raised-palm signal as together they'd spawn on top of each other every time you raise a hand. |

The booth blocks the second pick in each pair rather than letting them collide.

## 67 Mode (separate mode)

Switches the whole screen over to a 20-second exciting game. While it's running,

1. Press **Start**
2. Type in your name
3. Do 67 as fast as you can for 20 seconds
4. See your score and rank land on the leaderboard

Your name rides along with your score, so it shows up right next to it on the leaderboard.

## QR Code Mode (separate mode)

Instead of tapping the animations on screen, the booth can be switched to recognize a set of physical QR cards. Hold the card for a character up to the camera and that character locks onto you. It will keep following the participant that held the QR card, even once the card is put away or a photo is taken.

## Getting your photo

Once a photo (or strip) is confirmed, a QR code is printed right onto it. Scan that code with your phone to open an online copy of the photo you can download or share. You dont need to wait around for a print.

## Company logos

Two logo slots are set by the operator, not the participant, but you'll see both:

- **Banner logo** A company logo shown on screen over the live camera feed while you're in front of the booth.
- **Footer logo** A company logo printed in the footer of your photo strip, alongside the fixed event logo and the download QR code.

Which company's logo shows up can change between participants if the operator switches it.

## Good to know

- **Burst Mode** When it's on, three shots are taken back to back and printed onto one strip instead of a single photo.
- **The limit** Five looks is the most that can run at once.
